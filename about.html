About DokterWay

An aesthetic dermatology clinic dedicated to natural-looking, confidence-boosting results.

Who we are

Aesthetic dermatology, done thoughtfully

DokterWay is an aesthetic dermatologist clinic that specialises in facial filler, botox, threadlift and face contouring. We combine medical expertise with an artistic eye so every treatment enhances your features rather than changing who you are.

From your first free consultation we listen, assess your skin and facial structure, and recommend only what you need — with clear explanations of options, results and aftercare.
